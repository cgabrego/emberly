FAQ

Good questions

How long does a charge last?

That depends on the heat level. Low lasts longest and high runs out fastest. For the longest time between charges, use the lower levels or run one warmer at a time.

How hot does it get?

There are three levels, from gently warm to toasty. Overheat protection keeps it in a comfortable range. Stop using it if it ever feels too hot on your skin.

How do I charge it?

Use the included USB cable with any standard 5V USB charger, power bank or computer port.

Is it safe to take on a plane?
